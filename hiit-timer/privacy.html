Privacy Policy - HIIT Timer

Effective date: 22 September 2026

Developer: Terence Wilkie
App: HIIT Timer (com.terence.hiittimer)
Contact: tryreachme@gmail.com

Summary

HIIT Timer is an offline workout interval timer and stopwatch. In version 1 it does not require an account, does not include ads or analytics SDKs, and does not send your workout data to our servers.

Data we store

Settings and preferences (for example presets, keep-screen-on choices, or local ratings history) are stored only on your device, typically via the app's local web storage (localStorage) / local app storage. This data stays on the device unless you clear app data or uninstall.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal information for accounts, analytics, advertising, or crash reporting in v1. We do not operate a login service for this app.

Notifications

On supported Android versions the app may ask for optional notification permission so it can show an ongoing local session summary while a timer or stopwatch is running (for example remaining time or that a session is active). These notifications are generated and displayed only on your device. They are not used for marketing, advertising, or remote messaging, and we do not send notification content to our servers.

Internet access

The app is designed to work offline after install. We do not use advertising or analytics networks in this version.

Children

HIIT Timer is a general fitness timer. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal data.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, cloud sync, ads, in-app purchases, or analytics, this policy will be updated before those features ship, and Play Console Data safety answers will be updated accordingly.
